Refunds

Refund policy

What this covers

Digital products (templates and guides), the Fee Confidence Audit, online courses and the PRICE Profit Diagnostic, sold by Into The Nest Ltd, trading as William Ringsdorf Consulting, including products purchased through designprofit.co.uk checkouts.

Digital products

Digital downloads are supplied immediately. Once the content has been accessed or downloaded the sale is final, because the statutory 14-day cooling-off right for digital content ends when supply begins with your agreement. Defective or undelivered products are the exception: contact us within 7 days of purchase with your order details and the problem is put right or refunded.

Fee Confidence Audit

The Fee Confidence Audit is delivered immediately on purchase and is not refundable. Your statutory rights where a product is faulty or not as described are unaffected.

Online courses

Refund eligibility varies by course and is stated on the relevant course page and at checkout. The Architect's Profit Accelerator carries the 30-Day Clarity Guarantee described at its checkout.

PRICE Profit Diagnostic

A full refund is available on request until your written report has been sent, and again within 14 days of your 60-minute call if you tell us the report gave you nothing you can use. The fee is also refunded if the report or the call is not delivered. Your statutory rights are unaffected.

Faulty or not as described

Contact us within 7 days with your order details and a description of the issue.

How to request

Via the contact page, or to info@williamringsdorf.com, with your order details.
