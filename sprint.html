The 12-Week Sprint · £1,999 + VAT

Twelve weeks. Ten practices. One job: the margin.

A cohort-based implementation programme for principal-led practices, application only, with an outcome guarantee that means the risk sits on this side of the table.

Apply for a place

How the twelve weeks run

Weekly group calls with the cohort, 1:1 onboarding at the start, and the PRICE Framework implemented step by step in your practice, not studied in the abstract. The work follows the five steps: pricing foundations first, then scope protection, invoicing, cash flow, and earnings. By week twelve the system is running, not planned.

The outcome guarantee

A defined profit uplift, or ten hours a month reclaimed, by week twelve. If the practice gets neither, the work continues at no further charge until it does. This is not a refund clause to fall back on; it is the structure of the engagement. The question changes from whether it works to how much it produces.

Ten practices, and that is the ceiling

Each cohort is capped at ten because that is the number for which weekly calls and proper 1:1 onboarding can actually be delivered by one strategist. The cap is operational, not theatrical. When a cohort fills, the next one opens when it opens.

Application, not checkout

The Sprint is applied for, not bought. The application leads to a conversation about your practice. Practices that fit are offered a place at £1,999 + VAT, or two payments of £1,050 + VAT. Practices that do not are told so directly, and pointed at the version of the work that fits better.
