This policy also covers our pages on designprofit.co.uk, the Fee Confidence Audit at audit.designprofit.co.uk, and the order, intake and booking forms those pages lead to.

What is collected

  • Name and email address, when you request the Profit Guide or use the contact form
  • Practice details you choose to share in the booking questions
  • Your name, email address and the time you chose, when you book a call through the calendar
  • Correspondence, when you write to us
  • Your name, email address and a record of what you bought, when you buy from us
  • The answers you give on the PRICE Profit Diagnostic intake form: your practice's name and size, your role, and its figures, including fee income, staff costs, overheads, what you took out of the practice, hours, fees quoted and money owed to you. Also any document you choose to upload with it, such as a fee proposal or accounts
  • Your name, your practice's name and your answers, when you take the Fee Confidence Audit
  • When you open one of our forms or the order page: the page address, where you came from and a session identifier, recorded by the form system
  • If you accept the advertising cookie, the information described under "Advertising cookie" below

We do not see or store your full card number. Stripe or PayPal handles your payment details. If you pay by card, Stripe keeps your card on file for our account after you pay. We do not use it to charge you for anything else.

How it is used

To deliver what you asked for, to reply to you, and for the email follow-up described at the point of signup. Your data is not sold. If you accept the advertising cookie, Meta receives the information described under "Advertising cookie" below and uses it under its own privacy policy.

Contact form and correspondence: to reply to you. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in answering people who write to us.

Profit Guide: to send you the guide and the follow-up emails described on the sign-up page. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We do this because you asked for it, and you can withdraw that at any time by unsubscribing.

Buying from us: to take payment, deliver what you bought, deal with any refund, and keep the records the law requires. We need these details to carry out our contract with you and to meet our legal duties.

PRICE Profit Diagnostic: your intake answers and any document you upload are used to prepare your report and the 60-minute call, and for nothing else. William Ringsdorf personally reviews and signs off every report before it is sent. Nothing from them is quoted or published without your written permission. Your booking details are used to arrange and hold the call. We need these to carry out our contract with you.

Fee Confidence Audit: your answers are used to produce your report on screen.

Advertising cookie

Our pages on designprofit.co.uk can use the Meta pixel, a piece of code from Meta that helps us see whether our adverts work and helps Meta choose who sees them. It loads only if you press Accept on the notice shown on those pages. If you press Decline, or press nothing, it does not load and the pages work the same.

If you accept, the pixel sets cookies in your browser and tells Meta which of those pages you view, along with details such as your IP address and browser. If you then fill in one of our forms or place an order, it also sends your email address and name (first name and surname, where the form asks for them) in scrambled (hashed) form, which lets Meta match it to a Meta account. Meta may connect this with your Meta account if you have one, and uses it under its own privacy policy. We do this only with your consent. You can change your mind at any time through the "Cookie choices" link at the foot of those pages.

We keep your choice in a small cookie of our own for up to 12 months, so that we do not ask you again on every page.

Where it is processed

Forms, the order page, the intake form, the booking calendar, the emails we send and the related contact records are processed by GoHighLevel. Its forms and pages load from its own servers and Google's cloud storage, with a typeface from Google Fonts and a security check from Cloudflare, which sets a short-lived security cookie. On the order page GoHighLevel sets a cookie of its own that lasts up to 12 months.

Payments are processed by Stripe, or by PayPal if you choose it at checkout. Each runs its own payment and fraud checks and may set its own cookies.

The 60-minute call is held on Google Meet, and the booking is kept in our Google Calendar. The call is not recorded.

Email to and from us is held in Google Workspace. An AI assistant from Anthropic reads incoming email to sort it and draft replies for us. Replies to our PRICE Profit Diagnostic emails are kept out of that and are read only by William Ringsdorf.

Emails we send through GoHighLevel pass their links through the servers of its email provider.

The Fee Confidence Audit runs on Vercel. To write your report, your name, practice name and answers are sent to an AI model provided by Anthropic. The app keeps a short-lived count of requests from each IP address, held by Upstash, to prevent misuse. We do not keep a copy of your answers or your report.

PRICE Profit Diagnostic reports are prepared using software provided by Anthropic, which processes your intake answers and uploaded documents on our behalf.

If you accept the advertising cookie, Meta receives the information described above.

The site itself is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, with DNS managed by Cloudflare. Its typefaces are served from the site itself, and it runs no analytics or advertising scripts. Pages that carry a form or the booking calendar load it from GoHighLevel as described above.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards the law provides for those transfers.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, you can object to how it is used, and where we rely on your consent you can withdraw it at any time. Requests via the contact page or to info@williamringsdorf.com.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can make a data protection complaint to us at the same address. We acknowledge complaints within 30 days. You can also complain to the ICO.

Retention

Personal data is kept for no longer than the law requires or the purpose needs. Enquiries, contact-form messages, booking details and pre-call answers are deleted once the matter is closed, and no later than 12 months after the last contact, unless an engagement follows.

PRICE Profit Diagnostic: your intake answers, any document you uploaded and our copy of your report are deleted 12 months after your report is sent, or sooner if you ask, unless further work with us follows.

Email sign-ups are kept until you unsubscribe, after which only a suppression record is held so that the opt-out is honoured. Records of paid engagements and purchases, including invoices and the correspondence needed to support them, are kept for six years after the end of the financial year in which the engagement ended, as UK tax and company law requires, and then deleted.
