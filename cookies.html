Cookies

This site

The site itself sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties; its typefaces are served from the site itself.

Embedded services

The booking calendar, and any forms embedded on this site, are provided by GoHighLevel and may set functional cookies of their own when those pages are used.

Our pages on designprofit.co.uk

These pages run on GoHighLevel, which sets a cookie of its own that lasts up to 12 months, and Cloudflare sets a short-lived security cookie. Meta's advertising cookie is used only if you press Accept on the notice shown there, and you can change that choice through the "Cookie choices" link at the foot of those pages. Your choice is kept in a cookie for up to 12 months.

Analytics

This site does not use Google Analytics or any other analytics or tracking service. The previous version of this site did; that has not been carried over.

Managing cookies

Browser settings let you block or delete cookies. Blocking the functional cookies set by the booking calendar or embedded forms may stop them working.
